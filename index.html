Our Story

Crafting Soulful Spaces

Founded by Ananya Sharma, Aura Design Studio was born from a passion for creating spaces that resonate with the homeowner's personality while maintaining a high standard of luxury and functionality.

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that a home should be a sanctuary—a reflection of one's journey and aspirations. We combine traditional Indian craftsmanship with contemporary global aesthetics to deliver interiors that are both grand and intimate.

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

+

Project timelines vary based on scope. A modular kitchen typically takes 4-6 weeks, while a full home interior project can take 3-5 months from design to handover.

Do you provide 3D visualizations?

+

Yes, we provide high-quality 3D renders for all major areas of your project to help you visualize the final outcome before execution begins.

What is your design fee structure?

+

We offer both percentage-based fees for turnkey projects and fixed design fees depending on the project's complexity and size. Contact us for a detailed quote.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

+

Absolutely. We specialize in blending existing pieces with new designs to create a cohesive and refreshed look for your home.

Do you offer post-installation service?

+

Yes, we provide a comprehensive warranty on our workmanship and modular units, along with dedicated support for any maintenance needs.
